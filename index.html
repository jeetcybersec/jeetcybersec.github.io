01. About Me

Who I Am

I am an Offensive Security Researcher passionate about uncovering critical vulnerabilities across web applications, APIs, and networked infrastructure. With a manual-first approach, I focus on realistic attack simulation and business-impact-driven exploitation — prioritizing signal over surface-level noise.
